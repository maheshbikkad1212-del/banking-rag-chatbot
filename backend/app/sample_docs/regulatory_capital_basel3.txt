Document Name: RBI Prudential Framework on Basel III Capital Adequacy & Liquidity Ratios
Authority: Reserve Bank of India Department of Regulation
Effective Date: 2024-04-01
Version: 5.0
Category: Prudential Regulation

SECTION 1: MINIMUM CAPITAL ADEQUACY RATIOS (CRAR)
1.1 Capital to Risk-Weighted Assets Ratio (CRAR):
- Commercial banks in India must maintain a minimum Total CRAR of 9.00% of Risk-Weighted Assets (RWA).
- Including the Capital Conservation Buffer (CCB) of 2.50%, the minimum total capital requirement is 11.50%.

1.2 Tier 1 & Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) Limits:
- Common Equity Tier 1 (CET1) Minimum: 5.50% of RWA.
- Total Tier 1 Capital Minimum: 7.00% of RWA.
- Additional Tier 1 (AT1) Capital Ceiling: Up to 1.50% of RWA.
- Tier 2 Capital Ceiling: Up to 2.00% of RWA.

SECTION 2: LIQUIDITY COVERAGE RATIO (LCR) & NET STABLE FUNDING RATIO (NSFR)
2.1 Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR):
- Banks are required to maintain a minimum LCR of 100% on an ongoing basis. High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) must be sufficient to cover total net cash outflows over a 30-day stress period.

2.2 Net Stable Funding Ratio (NSFR):
- Minimum prescribed NSFR is 100%. Available Stable Funding (ASF) must equal or exceed Required Stable Funding (RSF) over a 1-year horizon.
