SECTION 1: MANDATORY KYC DOCUMENTS FOR RESIDENT INDIVIDUALS
1.1 Officially Valid Documents (OVD) for Identity & Address Proof:
- Valid Indian Passport (unexpired)
- Aadhaar Card / e-Aadhaar with masked UID number
- Permanent Account Number (PAN) Card or Form 60
- Voter ID Card issued by Election Commission of India
- Driving Licence (unexpired)
- NREGA Job Card duly signed by an officer of the State Government.

1.2 High-Risk Customer Periodic KYC Re-verification:
- High-Risk Customers: Mandatory re-verification every 2 years.
- Medium-Risk Customers: Mandatory re-verification every 8 years.
- Low-Risk Customers: Mandatory re-verification every 10 years.

SECTION 2: NON-RESIDENT INDIAN (NRI) ACCOUNT ONBOARDING NORMS
2.1 NRE vs NRO Account Distinction:
- Non-Resident External (NRE) Account: Maintained in Indian Rupees, fully repatriable outside India. Interest earned is completely tax-exempt in India under IT Act Section 10(4)(ii).
- Non-Resident Ordinary (NRO) Account: Used to hold income earned in India (rent, dividends, pension). Interest is subject to Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) at 30% plus applicable surcharge.

2.2 Mandatory Documents for NRI Account Opening:
1. Copy of valid Passport displaying visa stamps and departure details.
2. Proof of NRI status: Valid Employment Visa, Work Permit, Residence Permit, or Overseas Student Visa.
3. Overseas Address Proof: Foreign Utility Bill (Water/Electricity), Bank Statement from overseas bank (less than 3 months old), or Employer Certificate.
4. Permanent Account Number (PAN) or Form 60.
5. Overseas Tax Identification Number (TIN) or SSN as per FATCA / CRS regulations.
6. All foreign documents must be self-attested and attested by an Indian Embassy / Consulate, Notary Public, or Overseas Branch of an Indian Scheduled Commercial Bank.
